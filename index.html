Quick formula reference

Ohm's law

V = I × R

Voltage (V) = current (I) × resistance (R). For a simple resistor circuit, current in amperes is voltage in volts divided by resistance in ohms.

LED current-limiting resistor (concept)

R = (Vsource − VLED) / I

Use the actual LED forward voltage and a safe current from the component datasheet. Check power ratings and polarity; do not connect an LED directly across a supply without suitable current limiting.
